Introduction
One of my favorite movies is The Conjuring. It is a horror movie released in 2013 and directed by James Wan. The story follows paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren as they help a family experiencing frightening supernatural activities in their farmhouse.

The movie is inspired by the paranormal investigations of Ed and Lorraine Warren. It shows how the family experiences strange and terrifying events inside their home.
